## 🧸 The big idea

The cloud is renting pieces of giant data centres by the minute. You learn the building blocks (compute, storage, network, identity) and how to stay secure and on budget.

## 🎯 By the end of this month you can…

- Explain regions, availability zones and the shared-responsibility model.
- Use IAM with least privilege.
- Choose between EC2, Lambda and containers for compute.
- Use S3, RDS, CloudFront, Route 53 and load balancers.
- Design a VPC and control cost.

## 📚 What you will study

- **Foundations:** Regions · Availability zones · IAM · Cost management
- **Compute:** EC2 · Lambda · Load balancers
- **Data:** S3 · RDS · CloudFront
- **Network:** VPC · Route 53
