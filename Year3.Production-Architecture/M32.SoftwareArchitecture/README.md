## 🧸 The big idea

Architecture is city planning for code: where the roads, districts and walls go so the city can grow for years without becoming a traffic jam. From now on you design first, then build.

## 🎯 By the end of this month you can…

- Apply SOLID, cohesion and coupling to real code.
- Compare layered, clean and hexagonal architecture.
- Decide between a modular monolith and microservices.
- Use DDD: bounded contexts, aggregates, ubiquitous language.
- Record decisions with ADRs and C4 diagrams.

## 📚 What you will study

- **Principles:** SOLID · Separation of concerns · Cohesion · Coupling
- **Styles:** Layered architecture · Clean Architecture · Hexagonal architecture · Modular monoliths · Microservices
- **Domain:** Domain-driven design
- **Communication:** ADRs · C4 diagrams

## 🏗️ Month project: Architecture Design Exercise

Instead of "build a feature": design the architecture for a given business problem, then refactor the SaaS toward a modular monolith.

- [ ] Context map
