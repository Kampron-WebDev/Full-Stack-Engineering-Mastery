## 🧸 The big idea

Observability puts windows and gauges on your system, like a car dashboard. When something breaks you can see why instead of guessing.

## 🎯 By the end of this month you can…

- Emit structured logs with correlation IDs.
- Define and collect metrics; build dashboards.
- Trace requests across services with OpenTelemetry.
- Write actionable alerts, health checks and SLOs.

## 📚 What you will study

- **Signals:** Logs · Metrics · Traces
- **Operations:** Alerts · Dashboards · Health checks · SLOs
- **Tools:** OpenTelemetry · Application monitoring · Structured logging · Error tracking
